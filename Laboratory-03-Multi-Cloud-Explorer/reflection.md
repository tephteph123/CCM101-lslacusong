# Mission Reflection

For me, the most impressive cloud platform was AWS. I found
useful AWS because it provides a lot of cloud services for computing, storage,
networking, databases and other business needs.  
Learning about its services helped me understand how businesses build
and manage systems through the cloud.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three offer
cloud services via the web. They provide computing power, storage, databases,
networking, security and monitoring tools. Each platform also provides
enterprises with flexible resource management options which depend on the
workload and budget. The main differences are their service options, pricing,
tools, and overall platform features.

Business requirements strongly affect cloud platform
selection. A business needs to consider its budget, workload, security
requirements, storage needs, performance, and existing technology. For example,
a company with a limited budget needs to compare service costs before choosing
a provider. A company with specific security or application requirements also
needs to check which platform offers the most suitable services.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me to look at
cloud platforms based on actual business needs. I learned how to compare
services, identify suitable resources, and explain why a specific platform fits
a business situation. The mission also helped me improve my decision-making and
technical communication skills.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved after
completing this mission because I added more organized documentation and
practical outputs. The activities gave me evidence of my understanding of cloud
infrastructure, cloud platforms, and business requirements. My portfolio now
shows both my technical work and my ability to explain cloud solutions clearly.
